Archivo, IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono, Latin subsets, served from this site rather than from
Google Fonts.

Three reasons, in order of weight:

  1. fonts.googleapis.com is unreachable from mainland China. A render blocking stylesheet on an
     unreachable host does not degrade, it hangs, and the page stays blank until the connection
     times out. Two of this site's nine languages are Chinese.
  2. A German court (LG Munchen I, 3 O 17493/20) held that embedding Google Fonts transmits the
     visitor's IP address to a third party without consent, contrary to the GDPR. German is one of
     the nine.
  3. A library whose whole claim is that verification needs no network call should not call anyone
     else's server to draw its own headline.

Both families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which permits redistribution.
The licence text is in OFL.txt beside this file.

  Archivo         Omnibus Type            https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Archivo
  IBM Plex Sans   IBM Corp.               https://github.com/IBM/plex
  IBM Plex Mono   IBM Corp.               https://github.com/IBM/plex

The files here are unmodified Latin subsets as published by Google Fonts. The other subsets
(Cyrillic, Greek, Vietnamese, Latin Extended) are not carried, because none of the nine languages
on this site needs them: English, Portuguese, Spanish, French and German all fit inside Latin-1,
and the Japanese, Korean and Chinese pages fall back to the reader's own system font, which is
better than anything that could be shipped for them at a sane size.
